Privacy policy

How Giray Research Backup uses Google Drive access, encrypts backup files, retains data, and provides owner control.

Effective date: October 12, 2026 · Operator: Individual owner of Giray Research Backup

1. Purpose and operator

Giray Research Backup is an independently operated personal backup workflow, not a public service. It uses restic to encrypt and restore selected files and rclone to communicate with the operator's Google Drive account. This website is informational and does not accept user sign-ins or file submissions.

2. Google information accessed

The workflow requests the OAuth permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, allowing it to work with files made available to the application, including encrypted backup repository objects. It may also access Drive file/folder metadata and account storage-quota details to confirm account and repository identity. It does not request Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, or unrestricted full-Drive access.

3. Why the information is used

  • Authenticate the owner's Google account and validate the intended destination.
  • Upload and retrieve encrypted backup repository objects.
  • Inspect relevant metadata, check repository integrity and restore approved snapshots.
  • Report storage usage, error conditions and recovery status.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising or profiling, or shared with data brokers.

4. Encryption and credential protection

Selected source content is encrypted locally with restic before being uploaded to Google Drive. Restic recovery passwords are maintained separately from OAuth tokens and must be retained by the owner to recover data. OAuth tokens reside in access-restricted, host-local rclone configuration files. The encrypted repository and its Drive-side metadata are stored by Google; Google may process those objects and service metadata under its own terms and policies.

No client secrets, OAuth tokens, recovery passwords, or research files are intentionally published on this website. The website contains no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or external JavaScript.

5. Retention, deletion and access revocation

The owner decides which sources are backed up and when snapshots or repositories are removed. There is no automatic public-facing retention policy. Backup objects may remain in Google Drive until the owner deletes them. Removing the application's authorization from the owner's Google Account can stop future access but does not automatically erase previously uploaded encrypted objects. Deleting the repository data is a separate action managed by the owner.

6. Website visitors

These static pages do not intentionally collect form data, set tracking cookies, operate analytics or provide visitor authentication. The hosting institution may keep normal server access logs under its own policies; those logs are outside this personal application's control.

7. Sharing and access limitations

The intended user is the owner of the configured Google account. The workflow is not designed to share backup contents with outside users or advertisers. Any permitted third-party or institutionally controlled data must be assessed before inclusion in backup source policies.

8. Policy updates

This page may be updated if access permissions, software components, destinations or data-handling practices change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy or application questions, contact giraymecit+gouth_github@gmail.com.
